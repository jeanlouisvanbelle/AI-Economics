Authored by Jean Louis Van Belle & Google Gemini (October 2026).   📌 Repository OverviewThis repository contains the simulation code, analytical models, and visual assets accompanying two interconnected working papers hosted on ResearchGate:Paper I: Augmenting Romer's Endogenous Growth Theory for the Age of Cognitive Automation   Explores the macroeconomic consequences of direct cognitive capital substitution ($K_{AI}$).   Models senior human expertise as an endogenous, lagged accumulation of junior upskilling.   Evaluates digital border tariff vulnerabilities under the 1998 WTO Moratorium and GATS Article XVII.   Formulates an internal, revenue-neutral volumetric token tax shift ($\tau_{AI} \to \tau_p \to 0$) to restore factor-price neutrality.   Paper II: The Corporate Playbook for "AI-Augmented Apprenticeships": From Entry-Level Displacement to Sustainable Cognitive Design   Identifies the microeconomic "System Black Box Threshold" ($t^* \approx 4.5\text{--}5.2\text{ years}$) where unmanaged technical debt outstrips human diagnostic capacity.   Outlines three operational archetypes: Adversarial Audits, Sandboxed Iteration Tracks, and Dual-Prompt Audit Trails.   Establishes KPIs centered on Verification Density rather than raw volume.   📐 Theoretical Framework & Core Equations1. The CES Knowledge Production FunctionRomer's classical formulation $\dot{A} = \delta A L_A$ assumes technology only complements human intellect. We reformulate aggregate knowledge generation using a nested Constant Elasticity of Substitution (CES) aggregator:   $$\dot{A} = \psi A^\phi \left[ \alpha L_{senior}^\rho + (1 - \alpha) \Big( \omega L_{junior}^\sigma + (1 - \omega) \Phi_{AI}^\sigma \Big)^{\frac{\rho}{\sigma}} \right]^{\frac{1}{\rho}}$$Inner Elasticity ($\sigma \to 1$): High factor substitutability between entry-level execution hours ($L_{junior}$) and automated token flows ($\Phi_{AI}$).   Outer Elasticity ($\rho < 0$): Strong complementarity between high-level human oversight ($L_{senior}$) and aggregate task output.   2. The Dynamic Law of Motion for Senior ExpertiseSenior human capital is not an exogenous, infinitely replenishable stock. It depends dynamically on the historical runway of junior training subject to a structural apprenticeship delay ($\lambda \approx 3\text{ years}$):   $$L_{senior}(t) = L_{senior}(0)e^{-\gamma t} + \int_{0}^{t} \theta \cdot L_{junior}(\tau - \lambda) \, d\tau$$When short-term cost optimization sets $L_{junior} = 0$, senior capacity decays exponentially.   3. The System Black Box Threshold ($t^*$)Let $H(t) = H_0 e^{-\gamma t}$ be institutional human capability and $C(t) = C_0 e^{r t}$ be deployed software complexity / technical debt. The critical threshold occurs when system complexity outpaces diagnostic human capacity ($H(t^*) = C(t^*)$):   $$t^* = \frac{\ln(H_0 / C_0)}{\gamma + r}$$Under standard industry attrition ($\gamma = 12\text{--}13\%$) and organic complexity compounding ($r = 12\%$), a corporate junior hiring freeze induces systemic operational insolvency in approximately 4.5 to 5.2 years.   💻 Simulation EnginesModel1.py: Baseline Analytical Synthesis EngineThe foundational macro/micro synthesis script demonstrating the crossover dynamics:   Scenario A (Unmanaged Defection): $L_{junior} = 0$, senior capability decays exponentially, and unvetted AI complexity grows at 12% annually, crossing at $t^* = 4.39\text{ years}$.   Scenario B (Playbook Steady State): Sustained apprentice intake balances retirements ($\theta L_{junior} = \gamma L_{senior}$), establishing an asymptotic dynamic equilibrium.
